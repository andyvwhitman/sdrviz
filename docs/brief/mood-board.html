Listening

Dark cockpit. Hairlines on near-black, dim cyan armed marks, one slow functional motion. Healthy is quiet, never green.

Keyed

Only the talking channel changes: orange frame, vertical glyph, promoted timer, live waveform. Same choreography everywhere.

Fault

Red on the faulted object only, plus one status line. Two 1 Hz pulses, then steady. ACK drops the red frame; red is present tense.

Motif library · functional accents

Every accent carries a scale, a position or a state. If you can't say what a circle measures, delete it. IDs match the brief, §6.

Channel tile · states, combinations, compact variant

M1 · M6 · M7 · M16 · M22 · M25 · §3.5
  • Standard 200 × 112. Squelch-open (diagonal glyph, brighter brackets) lasts 250 ms; then TX commits: full 2 px frame, 6% wash, AAR steps down to 13 px and the timer takes the right zone at 32 px.
  • Five layers resolve separately (brief §3.5): frame, glyph, status readout, fault reason (footer line) and ember underline. Example: recent + stale keeps the ember under a dashed frame. An acked fault keeps its red triangle and label, but the frame falls back to dashed.
  • Selection is an outer bracket set 4 px outside the tile, so it never collides with a TX or fault frame. Partial coverage shows M25 pips. Muted TX shows no flash and an outline meter. Compact 200 × 56 is used when a bank outgrows the standard grid.

Live key-up loop · demo

§7.3
  • Demo loop. It repeats here so you can study it. In the product it fires once per key-up, and a channel at rest does not move.
  • 0 ms: squelch opens, glyph goes diagonal, audio flows. 250 ms: commit with a 120 ms tx-hot flash. Release: 400 ms hold, then frame retracts and the ember underline remains (recent 2 min, then recent-dim to 10 min, compressed here). Every fourth cycle is a kerchunk that never commits.

Position-light glyphs

M6
  • State by arrangement, after the PRR position light native to the NEC. A grayscale screenshot must still read.
  • Dots r 2.5, pitch 6 px. TX and fault differ by shape because their luminance contrast is only 1.42:1.

Level meter · VU + peak hold

M16
  • 2 px bar on a −60…0 dBFS tick scale. VU ballistics of about 300 ms rise and fall keep it calm with speech.
  • The 1×8 px peak tick holds 1.5 s, then falls at 20 dB/s. Squelch closed: no bar, only a static 1×4 px floor tick updated every 10 s. Upper meter is a demo loop.

Feed site · range rings · ping

M4 · M5 · M14 · M23
  • The registration mark sits on the fictional feed site. Rings every 5 km are labelled at 10 km; the outer ring is the coverage radius (2 3 dash), hatched at 45°.
  • Demo loop: the ping repeats here only. In the product it fires once per TX commit, for every receiving feed (1200 ms). There are no continuous sweeps on the map.

Spectrum · graticule · squelch · dimension

M2 · M3 · M11 · M12 · M17
  • Scope-style graticule with the scale per division in a corner, not on every line. Frequency rises left to right (P8).
  • The dashed squelch line is the only threshold: it goes solid when crossed, audio opens, and TX commits 250 ms later. The glow is the two-stroke trick, on the TX slot only (12% plot wash).
  • The dimension line measures the keyed channel's 12.5 kHz NFM bandwidth. The cursor readout sits at the canvas edge.

Waterfall · TX slot overlay

§3.3
  • Lightness carries power and hue carries state. Only the keyed slot gets the 1 px orange outline and the 12% plot wash (wash-tx-plot; no text on it).
  • 2 rows/s, so 120 px is 60 s. The floor is clamped just below noise, so a quiet band looks quiet. One feed is shown at a time (feed selector). Pattern is SIM.

Waveform strip → transcript row

M15 · M24
  • One object from live to archive. It scrolls in orange while keyed, then freezes at key-up, turns line and becomes the clip thumbnail in History.
  • While decoding: a dashed bar, 8 px per second of audio, plus DECODING. No fake glyphs. On final text: a cosmetic word-by-word reveal capped at 400 ms total. Seed row tx-004; the low-confidence word (ink-3) is SIM. Demo loop.

Train data block · milepost · coordinates

M8 · M9 · M11
  • Seed row tx-039: MARC 410 calls "approach medium, Odenton, track 2" at 07:15:00, so the tag reads NEC 113.6 · CH54. NEC mileposts fall toward Baltimore (Amtrak's Philadelphia-based count). MARC 412 is SIM.
  • The leader repositions to 8 offsets (click or [/]; drag optional). The M9 tag is 32 × 48 with the railroad's prefix on top. Tail fades over about 600 m. The train is orange only while its crew is talking.

CTC track strip · lever row

M18 · M21
  • One schematic row per route (brief §9.0.4). The NEC · RF&P row runs Quantico → Washington → Perryville, with an MP-equation mark at the change of count. The Capital Sub ties in at Washington. The snapshot is 07:15:00: MARC 410 talking (seed tx-039), and the other occupancies are SIM.
  • Occupied blocks are 2 px line; the talking block is orange. Detectors are open diamonds. Freight is never drawn, because it is not tracked. Red is never used for occupancy.
  • The lever row gives per-channel mute, solo and record, with a lit pip above each lever that is on. Visual 16 px, 11 px letters, hit area 24 px.

Title block

M10
  • Sits bottom-right on every screen, like a drawing's sheet number. Labels are ink-3, values mono ink-2. The clock follows the selected zone (EST/EDT or UTC). SIM while fixtures are in use. The Map adds a fifth cell: © OpenStreetMap contributors.

Stringline · trains × time × radio

M19
  • Route r-nec, native NEC mileposts falling left to right (Washington 135.7 → Martin State 84.0). Y is time running down. Seed ticks: MARC 410 at Odenton (tx-039) and MARC 418 at Martin State (tx-042). Margin rail: CH20 tx-038 and CH22 tx-041. Strings and the other ticks are SIM.
  • The selected train is white at 2 px. The now-line is cyan, never red. This is the History signature.

Ridgeline · 24 h airtime

M20
  • Craft's CP 1919 stacked-line plot, as inverted by Saville. One ridge per channel at 10-minute bins, each filled with bg-void (the plot surface) for hidden lines. Up to 16 ridges, then OTHER.
  • The morning and evening commuter rushes must rise visibly. This is the Stats hero. The shape and the total are SIM, illustrating the data/sim/ density target.
05

Motion · the key-up choreography

The same sequence on every channel, on every screen. It is the "scan, track, connect, populate, animate" order rewritten for radio. Easing: cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1).
Build-on on app start and screen change only. Frame 0–200, grid 150–350, labels 300–450 and values 400–600 ms. A 20 ms tile stagger, capped at 600 ms total. Never on data updates, bank switches or tab return.
One ambient motion per screen. Live Ops: the 8 s sweep over the spectrum only. History: the playhead while playing. Data motion (waterfall, trains, pings) moves at data rate. At idle only the sweep, the waterfall, the smoothed spectrum and the header clock move.
Fault. Two pulses at 1 Hz, then steady. No alternation, ever: the reason has its own slot. ACK fades the frame once, over 400 ms. Under reduced motion, every state change is instant.
